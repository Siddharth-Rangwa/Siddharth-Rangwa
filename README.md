## 🧠 What I'm building

- 🔐 **Biometric authentication platform** — checks identity in 4 ways: iris, palm, face, and voice
- 📞 **AI voice agent** — answers real customer calls in 10 languages, powered by OpenAI LLMs and ElevenLabs STT/TTS, and lets callers interrupt it like a normal conversation
- 🎥 **CCTV analytics engine** — YOLOv8 finds people in live camera feeds, DeepSORT tracks them, and FaceNet face matching marks attendance automatically

> I like taking models from research to production — preparing the data, training the model, making it smaller and faster, and building the APIs that serve it.
